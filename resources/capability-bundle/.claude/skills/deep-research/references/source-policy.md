# Research source policy

Rank sources for each claim, not by brand alone:

1. Primary official documentation, law/regulation, original paper, first-party dataset, direct filing.
2. High-quality independent technical or investigative analysis with transparent evidence.
3. Reputable reporting that identifies sources and event dates.
4. Community reports for discovery or lived experience, labeled accordingly.

Track publication date separately from the date an event occurred. Triangulate material claims when sources have incentives or uncertainty. Do not manufacture consensus from duplicated reporting. Quote sparingly; summarize within copyright limits.

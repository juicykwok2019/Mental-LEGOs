# Evidence-led event review

Build a timeline with five columns: observable event, learner response, evidence reference, immediate consequence, confidence.

Then examine:

1. Which question or pressure cue appeared?
2. Which semantic/logical/language modules were retrieved?
3. Where did retrieval fail, and what alternative explanations remain?
4. What did the counterpart actually do or say afterward?
5. Which learning asset should be retained, revised, or tested again?

Do not equate fluency with effectiveness. A pause may be strategic, a disfluency may coexist with a strong decision, and a favorable outcome does not prove every response was good.
